How the 90 minutes
actually work.

No prep deck. No homework. No slide deck to review afterward. You bring one real decision or challenge that's been stuck — something with real stakes and real constraints. We work through it together, using AI as a thinking partner.

STEP 01
Map current AI usage
We take stock of where AI is already touching your work — tools, habits, workarounds — without judgment. This is the baseline. Most leaders are surprised by how much they're already doing and how little of it is intentional.
STEP 02
Clarify business priorities
We identify the two or three decisions or outcomes that actually matter most right now. Not what's on the roadmap — what's actually keeping you up at night. This gives us a clear target for the session.
STEP 03
Identify decisions and constraints
We map the real constraints: time, team, data, risk tolerance, organizational dynamics. This is where most AI advice falls apart — it ignores the actual operating conditions. We don't.
STEP 04
Diagnose where AI supports judgment — and where it creates risk
This is the core of the session. We work through the specific decision together, using AI as a thinking partner in real time. You see exactly where it helps, where it misleads, and why.
STEP 05
Define next orientation: what to change, ignore, or test
You leave with a clear next move — not a list of tools to try, but a specific action tied to the decision you brought in. And a mental model you can apply to the next decision on your own.
What People Bring

Real decisions.
Real constraints.

These are the kinds of decisions that come into the room. Not hypotheticals — real situations with real stakes.

The build vs. buy decision in AI is rarely about technology. It's about where your real competitive advantage lives, what your team can actually sustain, and what the vendor landscape actually looks like right now. We work through the specific context — your team, your data, your timeline — and come out with a clear recommendation you can defend.

Most leaders have a stack of tools they've tried and a vague sense that they're not using them well. We map what you're actually doing against what you're trying to achieve, and identify the two or three things that would actually change your work — and the ten things that are just friction.

Adoption problems are almost never technology problems. They're judgment problems — people don't know which decisions AI can actually help with, so they default to not using it. We work through the specific team dynamics, the specific resistance, and the specific workflows to find the adoption path that actually fits your organization.

Most AI investment decisions are made with incomplete mental models. The question isn't just "will this work?" — it's "what does success actually look like, and how will we know if we're getting there?" We stress-test the decision, identify the assumptions that are doing the most work, and surface the risks that aren't in the vendor deck.

Governance frameworks that slow everything down are usually frameworks that don't understand the actual risk profile of the decisions being made. We work through your specific context — your industry, your data, your team's judgment — and build a governance approach that's actually proportionate to the real risks.
