How I Think About This

We're Not in the Digital Era Anymore.

You can't adopt AI the way you rolled out Salesforce or SAP. This isn't about which buttons to push. AI changes how work gets done at a fundamental level. The shift isn't from "not using AI" to "using AI." It's from doing the work to redesigning the work.

Letting go of habits that built your career. Watching AI do in seconds what took you years to master. Figuring out what you're worth on the other side of that. Most people navigate that alone. Danoosh helps leaders move through it with clarity, not just about what AI can do, but about what they'd attempt if they believed it could amplify what they already do well.
